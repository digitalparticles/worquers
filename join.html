02 / YOUR INTRODUCTION

ONE PAGE.
ONE EMAIL.

Keep it short and professional. Attach a one-page PDF résumé and include the following details in your email.

  • Name and contact information — your full name, phone number, and current city and state.
  • Experience and skills — your years of experience, main trade, technical skills, relevant certifications, and team leadership experience.
  • Work authorization — confirm whether you are legally authorized to work in the United States.
  • Availability — your earliest possible start date, any scheduling restrictions, and how long you're available for assignments.
  • Travel preferences — confirm whether you're available for nationwide projects or prefer specific states or regions.
  • One-page PDF résumé — summarize your relevant work experience and recent projects. A professional photo is optional.
Please do not email Social Security numbers, identity documents, passports, or work authorization documents. A résumé and a simple confirmation of work authorization are sufficient for your initial introduction.
